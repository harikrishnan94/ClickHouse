## Findings

### 1. Process artifact leaked into a test comment — `gtest_join_slot_routing.cpp:183`
`/// PREREG 001 check 4: max/mean slot fill < 1.5 at 1M rows over 256 slots, ...`
`PREREG 001` refers to `tmp/chj_probe_parity/PREREG.md`, a local workflow document that will not
ship. To the next reader this is a dangling citation and an obvious generated-workflow tell.
**Suggested rewording:** `/// Require max/mean slot fill < 1.5 at 1M rows over 256 slots, for both sequential and random keys.`

### 2. The build/probe contract is restated five times — header and cpp
- `JoinSlotRouting.h:21-23` — "The build scatter and the probe dispatch consume ONE shared fold implementation, and the word is a build/probe contract: the fold chain must not change without changing both sides in lockstep..."
- `JoinSlotRouting.h:84-86` — "The contract-pinning entry point: ... and tests compare its output against these words."
- `JoinSlotRouting.cpp:95-97` — "The words are a build/probe contract: ... must not change without changing both sides in lockstep." (near-verbatim repeat of the header)
- `JoinSlotRouting.cpp:130-131` — "...bit-identical to the column-outer accumulation, preserving the build/probe contract."
- `JoinSlotRouting.cpp:202-204` — "...the per-row fold chain ... is the same as a row-outer loop would produce."

A maintainer states the invariant once, in the header, and then keeps the fast-path comments to
the one fact each needs. **Suggested rewording:** keep `JoinSlotRouting.h:16-28` as the single
statement of the contract; reduce `JoinSlotRouting.cpp:95-97` to the part that is new information
(the sink is inlined; both entry points share this implementation); reduce the two fast-path
comments to "bit-identical to the reference fold chain". Also, `JoinSlotRouting.h:84-86` talks to
the reviewer about the test suite ("tests compare its output against these words") rather than to
the next reader — drop that clause; the tests speak for themselves.

### 4. Cargo-culted style suppression — `JoinSlotRouting.cpp:139`
`std::vector<FixedSource> sources; /// STYLE_CHECK_ALLOW_STD_CONTAINERS`. The std-containers lint
(`ci/jobs/scripts/check_style/check_cpp.sh`, `directories_to_lint_std_containers_usages`) does not

### 5. Unanchored "plan" vocabulary — `JoinSlotRouting.h:18`, `gtest_join_slot_routing.cpp:163`
"A plan of `bits` slots routes a row to slot..." and `TEST(JoinSlotRouting, SlotIdsAgreeWithWordsForEveryPlan)`.
"Plan" for a slot count is private design-doc vocabulary — `ConcurrentHashJoin` says "slots"
throughout, and in this codebase "plan" means query plan. **Suggested rewording:** header — "For
`bits` route bits, a row goes to slot `word >> (32 - bits)` (MSB-first)."; test —
`SlotIdsAgreeWithWordsForEveryBitCount` (or `...ForAllSlotCounts`).
