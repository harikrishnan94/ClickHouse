  `foldBytes`) inlined into the header, vs the reference's shared `ColumnsScatter.h` — justified:
  no `ColumnsScatter` module exists on this branch (repo-wide grep for the four names hits only
  the new files), and the gtest reference chain consumes them directly, which *is* the
  contract-pinning mechanism.

  (`ConcurrentHashJoin.cpp:177`, `toPowerOfTwo(min(slots, 256))`).

  dropped, not ported — correct surface-shrinking: the pure scatter design stores no per-row
  routes and has no lane sketch.

  transitively) — an improvement, kept.
- The reference's measured-perf receipts were dropped from two comments ("~4x the memops …
  measured 6.6% of the probe query's CPU", "measured 11-19% of the probe query's cycles") while
  the shapes those measurements justify were kept — see open question.
