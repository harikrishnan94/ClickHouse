## Leads (UNSETTLED — reported, not accepted; not rows because their commits are owned above)

- **L1 per-leaf HLL granularity**: phj5 sizes each leaf by its OWN sketch (per-worker x per-leaf registers fed on the final scatter pass); ahj rescales one global per-lane sketch uniformly and clamps by per-leaf rows (Build.cpp:1318-1328). Equivalent under route-hash uniformity; diverges under per-leaf duplication skew. Porting is architecturally awkward at ahj: the global estimate is needed BEFORE the partition plan exists, and per-leaf feeding would move sketch work into the scatter. Settling evidence: a skewed-duplicates benchmark showing leaf mis-sizing (heap_fallbacks/amac_ring_growths counters) attributable to the uniform rescale.
- **L2 in-ring match emission**: phj5's probe ring emits matches inside the ring (growable raw cursors, cold-outlined list walk); ahj's two-phase probe (find ring fills scratch; standard emit consumes) trades that for untouched join-kind semantics. Prior ahj campaign work names two-phase scratch as part of the parallel_hash gap (leads in tmp/probe_regression/). Porting = bypassing the shared emit machinery for at least inner ALL — a design change, not an incremental port; flagged for the requester rather than proposed.
- **L3 wide-width SWWC coverage**: phj5's appendGenericSwwc/appendStreamSwwc cover any multiple-of-4 element width; ahj's widthSupportsSwwc covers {1,2,4,8,16} and routes wider/irregular widths to direct copyRowExact. Only matters for wide fixed keys (FixedString(N), Decimal256) at high fanout; precondition-free (unlike fused records). No evidence of a hot shape today.
- **L4 user-tunable per-pass fanout cap**: RBM exposes radix_join_max_partitions_per_pass (default 1024, ceiling 8192); ahj hard-codes 1024. A knob, not a mechanism; note only.
