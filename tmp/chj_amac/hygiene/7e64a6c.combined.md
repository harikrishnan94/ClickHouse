- **Where:** `src/Interpreters/tests/gtest_concurrent_hash_join_amac.cpp:73–81`
  (forwarders), `:48` (`uintKeyBlock`), `:60` (`stringKeyBlock`).
- **Shape:** unearned indirection (delegate-only wrapper with one caller) →
  inline/collapse.
- **Evidence:** complete caller enumeration inside the anonymous namespace —
  `grep -n "uintKeyBlock\|stringKeyBlock"` finds exactly one caller of each:
  its `makeKeyBlock` forwarder (lines 75, 80). Every real call site
  (`:167,168,191,198,220`) goes through `makeKeyBlock`, and the two
  underlying functions already differ in parameter type
  (`std::vector<UInt64>` vs `std::vector<String>`), so plain overloading
  resolves every existing call, including the `std::vector<Key>{}` calls in
  `buildJoin`.

  through a forwarder pair that adds no behavior and no name value.

  `makeKeyBlock` and delete the two forwarders (lines 73–81); no call site
  changes.
- **Behavior preservation:** test-only, compile-time overload resolution is
  identical; the gtest itself is the coverage.
- **Severity x disposition:** nit / before merge.
