### 1. MINOR — "Chained hash maps" opener uses the wrong literature term
`src/Interpreters/ConcurrentHashJoin.cpp:522`

In standard hashing terminology a "chained hash map" is separate chaining —
where the described pathology (routing bits correlated with placement bits
stretching *linear-probe runs*) cannot occur, since chained tables have no
probe runs. ClickHouse's maps are open-addressing; the codebase's own term
for the probe sequence is "collision resolution chain"
(`src/Common/HashTable/HashTable.h:238`, and `FixedHashTable.h:100` contrasts
"It doesn't have a conflict chain"), which is what the comment leans on. A
reviewer who knows the literature stumbles on the first two words; one who
knows the codebase recovers late. Related imprecision on the same line:
"place a key at `hash & mask`" — under linear probing that is the *home*
cell, not necessarily the final cell; the comment itself switches to "home
cells" two sentences later.

Rewrite (first sentence and the `FixedHashMap` sentence):

```
/// Open-addressing hash maps put a key's home cell at `hash & mask` — the
/// same low bits a plain `hash & (num_shards - 1)` route consumes. ...
/// ... `FixedHashMap` types (`key8`/`key16`) index cells directly by the key
/// value and have no collision chains, so the low bits remain the natural
/// route for them.
```

(“Route chained types by …” on line 526 then becomes “Route grower-based
types by …” or simply “Route the remaining types by …”.)
